# Reconstruction methods draft

**Status: implementation and protocol draft, not a report of completed empirical experiments.** Sample sizes, performance estimates and inferential results must be supplied only after the reconstructed dataset is complete and the experiments have been rerun.

## Prediction origin and risk set

The unit of analysis is a firm at an annual prediction origin of 1 May. Predictor information is restricted to disclosures publicly available by the end of 30 April in China Standard Time. The prospective ascertainment window extends from the prediction origin through 30 April of the following year. The annual numerical branch contains five fiscal years, from Y−5 to Y−1. Four quarterly changes cover Y−1Q2 through YQ1 and require five quarterly base observations.

Eligibility is assessed at each prediction origin. A record is excluded when a qualifying distress condition is already established before prediction: ST or *ST treatment; negative consolidated net profit and negative operating cash flow in each of the relevant two consecutive quarters; or a non-standard financial-statement audit opinion accompanied by an annual consolidated loss. Consolidated net profit includes non-controlling interests. Internal-control audit opinions are not substituted for financial-statement opinions. Each component is coded as present, absent or unresolved. Any present component excludes the record; all three must be verified absent to pass the baseline screen. Unresolved records remain outside the analytical sample. Previously distressed firms are not permanently prohibited from re-entry, but must meet the same baseline requirements at the new origin.

The outcome concerns a qualifying event newly established during follow-up. The event date is the first public disclosure establishing the condition, rather than the accounting period-end. Formal ST implementation announcements and effective dates are stored separately. An announcement of possible future risk does not establish implementation. Excluded records receive no analytical outcome. A negative outcome requires complete ascertainment of all components throughout follow-up; missing evidence is not coded as an absence of distress.

## Public sources and numerical reconstruction

The reconstructed data are sourced from public issuer disclosures. The database retains company identifiers, document URLs, public disclosure dates, file hashes, report versions, physical PDF page numbers, accounting scopes and measurement units. Reported values are stored as exact decimal strings. Monetary values are normalized to renminbi, while the original values and units remain available. Single-quarter flow measures are derived from comparable cumulative statements and retain their raw-fact dependencies. Subsequently disclosed revisions cannot be backfilled into an earlier prediction origin. Missing or incomparable source values remain unresolved.

The missingness criterion is applied separately to the annual and quarterly input branches before imputation. The fixed denominators are 90 annual indicator-year values and 32 quarterly indicator-quarter values for each firm-origin record. A record is excluded if more than 30% of either branch is missing: at least 28 annual values or at least 10 quarterly values. Up to 27 annual and 9 quarterly missing values satisfy this screening component, subject to the remaining eligibility requirements. Source acquisition and review must be complete before this criterion is used; uncollected or unreviewed information remains unresolved rather than establishing an issuer-level missingness exclusion.

The full historical sampling frame, text corpus and event coverage are still being completed. Accordingly, no aggregate count from the lost dataset is treated as a property of this reconstruction. The current two-company database is a set of verification cases, not a representative analytical sample.

## Reconstructed model

The annual encoder maps 18 indicators to a 128-dimensional space, adds learned positions and applies two Transformer encoder layers with four heads, feed-forward dimension 256 and dropout 0.2. The quarterly encoder maps eight changes to 128 dimensions and applies a two-layer LSTM with dropout 0.3. The final annual and quarterly states provide the pooled numerical representations. A fixed revision of Chinese FinBERT supplies token-level text states, which are projected from 768 to 128 dimensions; its CLS representation provides the pooled text vector.

Three directional attention paths use the final annual state to query text states, the final quarterly state to query text states, and the final annual state to query the four quarterly states. Padding tokens are excluded. The three interaction outputs and three pooled representations are fused using sample-dependent softmax weights computed from their concatenation. The classifier applies hidden layers of 64 and 32 units with GELU, BatchNorm and dropout 0.3 before producing a binary logit. The implementation also retains a single-key interpretation of the manuscript for diagnostic comparison; its attention weights are identically one and do not provide query-dependent selection.

These modifications are newly implemented choices rather than evidence about the lost original program. The Chinese checkpoint's training-data cutoff is unknown; its use cannot establish historical deployability throughout the study period or eliminate potential overlap at pretraining time.

## Planned estimation and evaluation

All winsorization, industry median imputation and standardization parameters will be estimated using the training partition alone. Labels will not be imputed. The proposed chronological partitions contain prediction years 2017–2021 for training, 2022–2023 for validation and 2024–2025 for testing, with label-maturity checks at the fitting and model-selection boundaries. The implementation uses AdamW, separate learning rates of 1e−3 and 2e−5 for non-text and text parameters, weight decay 1e−4, focal loss with positive-class weight 0.75 and focusing parameter 2, a maximum of 80 epochs and validation-loss patience of 10. Batch size 32 and seeds 42–46 are new reconstruction defaults.

The best validation-loss checkpoint will be restored before test evaluation. Confusion counts and record-level probabilities will support exact metric reconciliation. Multiple seeds and paired firm-cluster bootstrap comparisons will distinguish optimization variability from conditional sampling uncertainty. Performance claims, significance claims and comparative conclusions remain pending until these experiments have been completed on the frozen reconstructed sample.
