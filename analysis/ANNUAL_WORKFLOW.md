# Annual ST audit and experiment workflow

The user authorized a smaller annual-report-only design and a complete eligible subset of the available source data, with no fixed sample quota. This workflow removes the implementation gap between collection and estimation. It does not presume the model improves performance.

1. `python analysis/audit_annual_inputs.py`: choose the latest preorigin full annual report from the complete filing index, including versions not yet downloaded. A missing newer report prevents substitution of an older report. Audit the downloaded selected reports against source codes, dates, explicit column headers, yuan units, consolidated scope, accounting identities, physical row anchors and MD&A boundaries. Interest expense, capex and quarterly comparability are not required by the 12-ratio design. Automatic checks remain labelled `rule_based_source_checks_not_manual_review`.
2. `python analysis/build_annual_st.py`: join manual and rule-based financial/text evidence to historical industry, listing status, baseline ST state and observed follow-up labels. Missing industry, unknown follow-up, ambiguous report versions and unresolved inputs cannot pass. The original manually reviewed facts are preserved.
3. `python analysis/freeze_annual_st.py`: export every eligible observation at this source-availability snapshot if every time split has at least two distinct firms of each class. This is a technical computation minimum, not a claim of statistical power. Otherwise record exact blocking reasons without creating a frozen manifest. Once frozen, the dataset is immutable. A later expansion requires a new version rather than automatic replacement.
4. `PYTHONPATH=src python -m distress.annual_train --root . --output runs/annual-st-v1`: identical sample, fitted text representation, preprocessing and three prespecified seeds across financial-only, text-only, concatenation and cross-attention models. Financial representations query multiple text chunks with padding masked. The concatenation head has exactly the same trainable parameter count as cross-attention. There is no gate experiment in this reduced design.

Preprocessing is fit on training observations only. MD&A is divided into 256-character chunks; at most 16 evenly spaced chunks cover the whole section. Character TF-IDF (1–2 grams, 20,000 vocabulary cap) and truncated SVD (up to 64 dimensions) use only training chunks. Financial ratios use training quantiles, medians and scaling plus missingness indicators. Adam trains at most 100 epochs with validation-AP early stopping (patience 15); classification thresholds maximize validation F1. No test-based model or sample selection is permitted.

All three seeds' test scores and curves are retained, with mean and seed standard deviation. Primary uncertainty is a 2,000-replicate paired company-cluster bootstrap of mean single-run test average precision, retaining repeated observations within each sampled firm. Comparisons against financial-only, text-only and concatenation use identical bootstrap draws. The confidence interval describes uncertainty conditional on the frozen source-availability sample; it does not establish representativeness or causality. Individual-run secondary metrics and confusion matrices are retained. Seed training logs, predictions and preprocessing/checkpoints are saved.

The publication must disclose source-availability selection, automatic extraction limitations, sparse positive events and any inconclusive comparisons. Historical industry gaps are not filled from today's industry. ST measures registered regulatory status transitions rather than all financial distress. Tests using synthetic fixtures validate software only and must never be reported as empirical results.

`Annual ST audit freeze and experiments` can audit existing archives immediately without waiting for new downloads. It commits audit results and any frozen manifest before estimation, then commits predictions, metrics and logs. The regular research pipeline also audits after extraction. Hashes and the same frozen summary are recorded in README, filing_summary and frozen_manifest. Failed commits retain recovery artifacts and do not silently overwrite remote data.

The v2 audit bounds the formal financial statements between explicit section headings before accounting notes. Thus an adoption adjustment table in the notes is not counted as a second formal balance sheet. Standard current/prior headers require the bounded annual statement context plus an explicit year-end balance-sheet date. Front-matter stock-code checks cover the first 15 pages because company information can follow a long table of contents. Reports labelled cancelled or withdrawn are ineligible. These rules apply uniformly to all outcomes and years.
